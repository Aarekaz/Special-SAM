\section{Conclusion}

We investigate how far decoder-only adaptation can take SAM for camouflaged object detection. Fine-tuning only the 4M-parameter mask decoder yields +16.6 mIoU on COD10K with no architectural changes, 90-minute training, and a 16MB checkpoint. The key finding is prompt robustness: mixed-prompt training reduces sensitivity to prompt type from 3.5$\times$ to 1.1$\times$, validated by ablation. A complementary 31K-parameter LLPM enhances boundary signals before the frozen encoder, adding +2.6 mIoU on edge prompts. Decoder-only fine-tuning does not match encoder-adaptation methods, confirming that decoder and encoder specialization address complementary bottlenecks. Cross-dataset evaluation on CAMO (+18.8 mIoU) and NC4K (+18.5 mIoU) shows the decoder generalizes without retraining. Error analysis reveals structured failure modes (dark images, tiny objects, boundary ambiguity), pointing toward encoder--decoder co-adaptation as the natural next step.
