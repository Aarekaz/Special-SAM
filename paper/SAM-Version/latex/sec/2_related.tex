\section{Related Work}

\noindent\textbf{Segment Anything Model.}
SAM~\cite{kirillov2023sam} introduced promptable segmentation trained on SA-1B; SAM2~\cite{ravi2024sam2} extended it to video. SAM-Adapter~\cite{chen2023samadapter} inserts learnable modules \emph{inside} the image encoder. COMPrompter~\cite{hao2024comprompter} replaces SAM's prompt encoder with a multi-prompt network. Liu~\etal~\cite{liu2025dualstream} attach dual-stream adapters. We differ in two ways: we fine-tune the \emph{existing} decoder without adding parameters, and the LLPM operates \emph{before} the encoder in input space rather than modifying the encoder.

\noindent\textbf{Camouflaged Object Detection.}
Dedicated COD architectures such as SINet~\cite{fan2020cod10k}, PFNet~\cite{mei2021pfnet}, ZoomNet~\cite{pang2022zoomnet}, and SegMaR~\cite{jia2022segmar} are designed from scratch. We take the opposite approach: start from SAM's pre-trained weights and ask how far decoder-only adaptation goes without any domain-specific architecture. Das~\etal~\cite{das2025camouflage} introduced synthetic camouflage generation, which addresses the data side and could be combined with our decoder-side adaptation.

\noindent\textbf{Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning.}
LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora} and prompt tuning~\cite{lester2021prompt} adapt large models by adding parameters to the encoder or prompt pathway. We freeze everything except the existing mask decoder, adding zero new parameters. The LLPM adds only 31K parameters before the encoder.
