\section{Conclusion}

Freezing SAM's ViT-H encoder and fine-tuning only the mask decoder is enough to gain +16.6 mIoU on COD10K, with no architecture changes and 90-minute training. Mixed-prompt training is the mechanism behind prompt robustness, cutting sensitivity across prompt types from 3.5$\times$ to 1.1$\times$. A small pre-encoder module (LLPM, 31K parameters) adds further gains on boundary-sensitive cases. The decoder generalizes to CAMO and NC4K without retraining, and its remaining failures cluster in interpretable categories (dark images, tiny objects, boundary ambiguity). Decoder-only fine-tuning does not reach the performance of encoder-adaptation methods, so encoder--decoder co-adaptation and automatic prompt generation are the obvious next directions.
